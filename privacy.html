Privacy and participation

Know what you are sharing.

Owner experiences and professional contributions are collected for project research. They are not applications for emergency help.

Page updated 10 November 2026

Who runs the project?

Waiting Room Stories is led by Ollie Cutts / KingChilliThePug. For a question about your contribution, public use, correction or deletion, email info@kingchillithepug.com with “Waiting Room Stories privacy” in the subject.

What the forms collect.

The owner form asks about your country, the care discussed, costs, insurance, barriers, outcome and what might have helped. It includes a written account and choices about public use and follow-up contact. The professional form asks about professional experience and practical improvements, with separate choices about excerpts and contact.

Do not include other people’s names, contact details, claim numbers or identifying case information in written answers. You do not need to send medical records or bills to take part.

The forms are hosted by Fillout. Connected response copies are stored in Google Sheets, with separate project review and export files in Google Drive. The owner and professional response Sheets checked on 10 November 2026 showed owner-only sharing permissions. That check does not establish every form setting, integration permission or backup copy.

This public website is hosted on GitHub Pages; the forms are separate from it. A response is not automatically published on the website.

Public use and quotations.

The owner form offers anonymous public use, contact before use, or private research only. Professional excerpt permission is asked separately. A choice to allow anonymous use is not permission to publish identifying details.

Anonymous reporting is different from anonymous collection: written details or optional contact information may still identify you within a response.

We do not publish full private submissions or personal identifiers. Any proposed quotation needs permission appropriate to that use and a check for identifying details. If contact is required and cannot be made, that is not permission to publish.

A research submission will not become a public fundraising case without separate informed consent and a future process designed for that purpose.

How the first statistical release uses responses.

The first owner-statistics release uses only current-form submissions recording “Yes, anonymously” to the campaign-use question. Contact-first and private-research-only responses are excluded from that release. It contains grouped direct answers, not written stories, names, contact details or submission IDs. Smaller categories are combined, and UK counts are reported separately. The published method explains the dates, counting and limits. Legacy entries and professional responses are not added to that denominator.

The wider privacy review is still completing the processing basis, full provider and integration access, international data handling, retention criteria and the process for handling requests across Fillout, Sheets and export copies. No fixed automatic-deletion period is claimed here. Publishing a limited aggregate is not a claim that this wider review is complete.

If you need clarification before submitting, please email Ollie first. There is no need to share sensitive documents to ask a privacy question.

Correcting or removing a contribution.

Email the project with enough non-sensitive information to help locate your response, such as an approximate submission date and the country selected. Please do not send a new form entry as a correction.

A fully anonymous response may be difficult to identify. Information that has already been irreversibly combined into an anonymous summary may not be separable afterwards. The project should explain what can be located and changed in response to your request.

For information about UK data-protection rights or how to raise a concern, see the Information Commissioner’s Office.

Common questions.

Can you help with my animal’s current treatment or bill?

No. Waiting Room Stories cannot arrange care, provide grants or pay veterinary bills. Please contact your vet or emergency hospital directly if your animal needs care now.

Can I contribute from the United States?

Yes. International experiences are welcome. The first reform work is in the UK; US research and proposals will need a separate stage.

Are you investigating individual vets or insurers?

The project is gathering experiences to examine systems and possible improvements. It does not adjudicate individual complaints or provide legal, veterinary, financial or insurance advice.

Does the website use tracking?

This version of the public site does not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels or marketing cookies. Hosting and external form services have their own data practices; see their information when you use them.
