## Removed or reduced

- Repeated milestone rails, animated counters and decorative report mockups.
- Duplicate four-route blocks and repeated “practical pressure point” explanations.
- Seven near-identical reform cards, long clinical treatment lists and stock-photo hero bands.
- Automatic chart rendering, headline hydration and dynamically replaced share captions.
- Upkeep support in every footer; one clearly labelled optional project-cost route remains.

## Retained

- Existing URLs and redirects, domain, static hosting, forms and approved share artwork.
- Chilli’s original photographs, the plum/cream/gold identity, separate professional route and non-blame purpose.
- No new accounts, database, dashboard, analytics tracker, fundraising service, framework or paid dependency.

## Remaining gaps

- The installed data/export version, consent filtering, historical comparability, duplicate policy and actual record eligibility still need a controlled methods review.
- Storage/access, processors/transfers, retention, lawful basis and the practical deletion process are not established by the public repository. The website does not claim that they are.
- No end-to-end live form submission or private row audit was performed. Form opening can be checked without sending synthetic owner stories into the live dataset.
- Numerical withdrawal affects current files, not public Git history or old caches. Do not describe it as making all historical data private.
- Share images are relatively large existing PNGs. They are lazy-loaded, dimensioned and confined to the toolkit. Do not regenerate approved art merely to change compression.
- Website visits still cannot be equated with completed forms. No tracking was introduced under this repair.
