The Waiting Room Stories Project

Emergency vet care should not depend on an audience.

When Chilli needed specialist treatment, millions of people could hear his story. Most owners facing an emergency do not have that reach.

We collect experiences of cost, insurance and payment barriers, and use them to develop practical proposals for better access to care. Our first focus is the UK.

This is a reform project. We cannot offer emergency funding or arrange treatment.

From experience to action

What needs to work better?

We want owners and the people delivering care to help answer practical questions.

The amount needed now

Can owners understand the treatment estimate, the payment due immediately and what might change?

Insurance at the hospital

Can the hospital and insurer explain direct-payment options and the amount an owner may still need to find?

The next step

Can referrals and support routes give people useful financial information before another urgent decision?

Why Chilli’s story matters

Skilled veterinary care. An extraordinary community.

Specialist veterinary professionals treated Chilli’s life-threatening neurological illness. Donations from his community, raised through GoFundMe, helped make that care possible.

The question that stayed with Ollie was simple: what happens to the animal whose owner has nobody to ask?

Read why the project began

UK first. US work to follow.

A place to start, with a wider purpose.

Ollie’s direct experience of UK veterinary care gives us a starting point. US and other international experiences remain welcome; a later US stage will need its own research and partners.

Where the evidence stands

Owner experiences can reveal questions worth investigating. They do not tell us how common a problem is among all pet owners.

Our numerical findings are on hold while we check counting methods, permissions and small groups.
