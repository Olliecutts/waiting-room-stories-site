For professionals

Help us understand what happens on your side of the desk.

Veterinary teams, insurance and payment specialists, welfare organisations and researchers can help explain what works, what fails and what a realistic improvement would require.

Open the Professional Insight form

Opens on Fillout. Please read how contributions are used and leave out information that could identify clients, colleagues or cases.

What would be useful?

  • Where do payment or direct-claim decisions become unclear?
  • What information would make an emergency referral easier to manage?
  • Which support routes actually respond in the time available?
  • Which proposed changes could add workload, delay care or create unintended harm?
  • What already works well and could be learned from?

Our first reform work will focus on the UK. Contributions from the US and elsewhere are welcome, with their country context kept distinct.

Professional insight has its own limits.

Professional responses stay separate from owner experiences. They do not add to an owner-story total or stand for the whole profession.

The current professional sample is too small for public percentage claims. Numerical publication is on hold while the method and small-group protections are checked.

Written contributions are not automatically approved quotations. Publication needs both appropriate permission and a check for identifying information.

Interested in a practical conversation?

If your organisation could help assess one of the four questions, please email Ollie with your role, country and the part of the process you work on. There is no need to send private case records.
