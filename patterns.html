What owners have told us.

235 submissions are included in this first checked snapshot. Each records permission for anonymous campaign use and is counted once.

Reviewed 10 November 2026 · Included responses: 24 May–7 July 2026

This is a self-selected collection of experiences, not a representative survey or independently verified clinical evidence. Most included responses are from the United States.

What we propose to test.

At one UK hospital, can an owner understand the amount payable now, the actual status of any insurance payment, and who will give them the next answer?

A proposed small trial would record unresolved payment barriers and staff time as well as owner understanding. A clearer explanation cannot, by itself, make an unaffordable bill affordable. No hospital or insurer has agreed to a trial.

Method and limitations

What the numbers represent.

  1. Source and dates. We reviewed the connected owner-response Google Sheet on 10 November 2026. Its current-form records run from 24 May to 3 September 2026. The included, public-use subset runs from 24 May to 7 July. The review date is not a claim that new stories arrived that day. Completeness against the live Fillout account has not yet been independently reconciled.
  2. Counting submissions. The sheet contains 295 rows with 294 distinct form submission IDs. One ID appears twice; its structured answers and permission match. We retained the latest timestamp. These are submissions, not proven distinct people, animals or emergencies. A person could use a new submission ID more than once.
  3. Publication permission. We included only the 235 submissions recording “Yes, anonymously” to “Can we use your story anonymously as part of this campaign?” The other 59 record contact-first or private-research-only choices and are excluded. Follow-up contact permission is a separate choice. This relies on the stored question and answer; the complete historical form notice has not been reconstructed.
  4. Direct answers only. No themes were added by scanning written accounts. Each submission counts at most once per displayed category. All included submissions answered the published questions, so each question has a base of 235 and no missing answers. Multiple selections can make category totals exceed 235. Percentages are rounded to one decimal place.
  5. Small groups. This release does not display category cells below five. We combine the smaller location categories; unclear-policy and reimbursement-wait barriers; “Other”/unsure care answers; and the insurance and cost-answer groups described beside their charts. UK figures are selected counts only, with no detailed cross-tabulations or individual records. This threshold is a precaution, not a guarantee of anonymity on its own.
  6. Checks and limits. We checked IDs, stored permission values, exact fixed-option parsing and arithmetic. No overt test marker or exact repeated full-content submission was found in the included set during a limited quality check. Experiences have not been clinically or financially corroborated. Self-selection, recall and the US-heavy audience limit interpretation. No population rates, causal claims, provider comparisons or price comparisons can be inferred.

The 41 legacy Google Form entries are kept separate from this release. Professional Insight is also separate; professional percentages remain unpublished while its small sample and release process are reviewed.

Why the earlier count changed.

The earlier total of 402 combined 361 rows in a processed table with a 41-entry historical aggregate. The processed table contained 279 distinct generated IDs, with 82 additional rows sharing those IDs. That does not mean 82 duplicate people.

This release uses the current form’s source records and its original submission IDs instead. The current source, older processed table and legacy series are different bases; subtracting permissions from 402 would not be a valid correction. The new count describes an explicitly defined public-use subset, not stories being deleted.

The old automatic chart refresh remains disabled. Each new statistical release needs its own checks and collection dates; this page is a fixed snapshot.
