Project update

From collecting stories to a UK-first campaign.

The next job is to turn experience into specific proposals and find out whether they can improve access to care.

Published 10 November 2026

A correction to the earlier Pattern Update.

This page replaces the earlier “250+ stories” update. That page loaded changing totals and percentages from the latest data file, so it was not a fixed historical report.

Those figures have been withdrawn from the current pages while counting methods, publication permissions and small groups are checked. This dated update contains no automatically refreshed statistics.

The first focus is the UK.

Ollie’s own experience of specialist veterinary care is in the UK. We will begin there, with questions about payment explanations, direct insurance payments, referral handovers and realistic support routes.

US contributors remain part of the project’s wider purpose. Their experiences will inform a separate stage; UK findings and rules will not be presented as US advice.

What happens next.

First, complete the evidence checks and prepare a short briefing that distinguishes owner experiences from independent research. Then seek professional feedback on a small, practical improvement that could be tested.

No trial or partnership is agreed. Future updates should report what was actually discussed or changed, including limits and unsuccessful approaches.

Read the four questions in our plan

The future fundraising ambition stays separate.

Eventually, we want to help owners without an audience have their genuine emergencies heard. That will need partners, verification, separate consent and clear responsibility for money and complaints.

Waiting Room Stories is not currently accepting emergency fundraising cases, paying veterinary bills or providing grants.
