For pet owners

Tell us what happened.

Did cost, insurance or payment arrangements affect an emergency or specialist-care decision? Your experience can help us understand the problem.

Open the owner story form

Opens the form on Fillout. Read how your response is used before submitting.

A few sentences are enough.

You do not need medical language or an exact bill. Tell us what care was discussed, what payment or insurance arrangements were explained, and what helped or got in the way.

  • Include your country so different systems can be understood separately.
  • Explain what worked as well as what was difficult.
  • Leave out names, contact details, claim numbers and other identifying information about other people.
  • If you have already shared the same experience, email us to correct it rather than sending it again.

Experiences from the UK, the United States and elsewhere are welcome. The first reform work will focus on the UK.

Your choices matter.

The form asks whether your experience may be used anonymously, whether you want to be contacted first, or whether it should stay private for research. Follow-up contact has a separate choice.

We are reviewing how those choices are applied before releasing further numerical findings. A story submission is not permission to turn your experience into a public fundraising appeal.

If your pet needs help now.

This form is for research. It is not monitored as an emergency service and cannot arrange care, provide money or secure funding.

Contact your vet or emergency hospital directly about your animal’s needs and the options available.
